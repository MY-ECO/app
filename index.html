ECO-AI - AI model for impacting the development

ECO-AI is an open research AI model designed to assist economists, policy analysts, public institutions, and PhD students in economic modeling, forecasting, and decision support.

The system focuses on transparency, explainability, and reproducibility while integrating modern machine learning techniques with established economic theory.

ECO-AI Whitepaper

The ECO-AI whitepaper documents the theoretical foundations, data assumptions, modeling choices, and evaluation methodology of the system.

It emphasizes the combination of econometric reasoning with machine learning pipelines, ensuring outputs remain interpretable and policy-relevant.

  • Macroeconomic & microeconomic grounding
  • Hybrid statistical–ML framework
  • Ethical AI and governance principles

Model Use Cases

  • Economic forecasting and scenario analysis
  • Public policy impact assessment
  • Academic research and hypothesis testing
  • Data-driven decision support for institutions
  • Educational assistance for economics students

AI Model Structure

ECO-AI is structured as a modular system composed of data ingestion, preprocessing, modeling, and explanation layers.

  • Data Layer: curated economic datasets & indicators
  • Model Layer: regression, probabilistic, and ML models
  • Interpretability Layer: explainable outputs & uncertainty bounds
  • Interface Layer: API & documentation-driven access

ECO-AI Compliance

ECO-AI is developed in alignment with responsible AI principles, academic integrity standards, and data protection guidelines.

  • GDPR-aware data handling practices
  • Bias awareness and mitigation strategies
  • Transparent documentation of assumptions

ECO-AI Versioning

ECO-AI follows semantic versioning to clearly communicate changes and improvements.

  • v1.0 – Initial public research release
  • Minor versions introduce model improvements
  • Major versions may change architecture or scope

ECO-AI License

ECO-AI is released under an open-source research license. Usage is permitted for academic, educational, and non-commercial purposes.

Commercial usage requires explicit permission from the project maintainers.

Disclaimers

ECO-AI provides analytical assistance only and does not replace professional, legal, financial, or policy advice.

Outputs are probabilistic and depend on data quality, assumptions, and modeling limitations.
